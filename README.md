# Dungeon Adventure

## About the Game

Dungeon Adventure is an action-packed dungeon crawler where players explore dangerous dungeons, fight enemies, defeat powerful bosses, and collect valuable loot. Players begin aboard a ship that acts as their main hub. From the ship, they can prepare for dungeon runs, equip weapons, customise their character, purchase supplies, and upgrade their equipment. As players progress, they can discover new areas, find better rewards, and take on increasingly difficult challenges.

## Features

- Player movement, including walking, running, and dodging.
- Character skins and customisation options.
- Animations for movement, combat, and attacks.
- Different weapons with unique attack styles.
- Dungeon maps with rooms, hidden areas, and treasure.
- Enemies and challenging boss encounters.
- Loot and rewards collected during dungeon runs.
- A ship hub for preparing between adventures.

## The Ship

The ship is the player's main hub and preparation area. It includes weapon storage, equipment selection, a character customisation area, a mission board, an item shop, a weapon upgrade station, and treasure storage. Players can use the ship to prepare their equipment and choose which dungeon to explore next.

## Gameplay

1. Start aboard the ship.
2. Prepare your character and choose your equipment.
3. Select a dungeon from the mission board.
4. Enter the dungeon and explore its rooms.
5. Fight enemies, avoid hazards, and collect loot.
6. Defeat bosses and complete dungeon challenges.
7. Return to the ship to use your rewards and prepare for another run.
